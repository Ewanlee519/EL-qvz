This is my mod for plants vs. zombies in Quake!

The first biggest change is that there is a new game mode which will take you to a custom map to play the game.
It is in the first person and it has a grid for you to place all of your plants. To pick your plant, simply
choose a button from 0-9 for your plant choice and place it down on the grid with the button P. 

One of the first features is the 10 monsters from Quake II that can now be used as plants:
1. Sunflower: (iron maiden)
2. Sunshroom: (technician)
3. Peashooter: (light guard)
4. Repeater: (machine gun guard)
5. Snowpea: (shotgun guard)
6. Cherry bomb: (flyer)
7. Potato mine: (flyer)
8. Wallnut: (Tank)
9. Tallnut: (Tank Commander)
10. Chomper: (Brains/mutant)

Another change are the behaviors for the zombies (berserkers) in this game. They can:
- Stay in one lane and stick to it
- Have a 1 in 10 chance of being a smart zombie who can switch lanes
- Spawn with different hats which indicate different types of zombies
- Constantly move forward
- There is also a type of zombie that is twice as fast

With the plants comes the sunlight economy which is used to buy plants and upgrade them into different tiers

Upgrades are also introduced in this mod and they are as follows:
- Lawnmower (kills all zombies in lane and can be used ONCE per lane)
- KaBOOM (destroys all zombies, 3 per game)
- Freeze zombies (freeze all zombies, 3 per game)
- Sunlight frenzy (for each sun plant, generate 100 sun, 3 per game, more for higher health plants)
- 5 free upgrades (3 min cooldowns)

This brings us to the last change: the 3 upgrade levels for the plants. Each upgrade namely increases 
the health of the plant and can increase the damage. There is the basic stage, then stage 1, then stage 2

Button Mappings:
TAB -> view the menu for all of the plants
0-9 -> choose your monster
P -> plant a monster in a spot
I -> inspect the stats of the plants
K -> dig up the plants for 1/4 sun back
F -> freeze power up
G -> sun frenzy power up (based on sun plants you have)
B -> kaboom power up
U -> upgrade power up
R -> activate hover lawnmowers for desperate measure
